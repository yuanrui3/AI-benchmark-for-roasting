# Roast Anything

A method for roasting any target the way the talk show 《人比AI更像AI》 does: dark, specific, built on verified facts, and aimed at behavior and hypocrisy rather than identity. Default language is natural spoken Chinese (stand-up register); write English only when asked.

## The core rule

谁最不许被调侃，谁先被调侃. The more a target insists it is above mockery, the higher it goes on the list. Sacredness is the setup; the gap between how the target sees itself and what it actually does is the joke.

## Workflow

1. **Name the target's self-image.** One sentence: what does this target believe about itself, or want you to believe? ("Europe: civilized, humane, better than America.")
2. **Find the facts that contradict it.** Search before stating anything about the current world: numbers, dates, direct quotes, court outcomes, settlements. Aim for 2-5 hard facts. A roast without a checkable fact is just an opinion. Prefer facts the audience doesn't know but can verify.
3. **Find the hypocrisy.** Put the self-image next to the fact. Look for: saying X while doing not-X; condemning in others what they do themselves; the same people switching positions when it costs them; a safe target only attacked because it is safe.
4. **Pick the angle, then the darker angle.** The first reasonable joke is usually the cliché. Ask: who else is implicated? Who profits? What does the audience do that's the same? Reasonable isn't funny; go one step darker than comfortable, but never cruel to bystanders.
5. **Write 2-5 beats that escalate.** Setup, （停一拍）, punch. Each beat should cut deeper than the last. End on a turn, not a summary.
6. **Turn it.** The best endings swing the blade somewhere unexpected: back at the audience, at the people who roast the target, or at the roaster herself.
7. **Self-check** (below), then deliver the roast with a short Sources list.

## Devices that work

- **Refusal as roast (不骂).** "X, I don't roast." Then the reason for not roasting is the roast. ("矮个子男人，不骂。全世界已经替我骂完了……我再骂就是落井下石。井不深，可他们照样露不出头。") Already-roasted-to-death targets are lazy unless they come with this twist.
- **Roast the roasters.** If everyone mocks a target, mock the people who only dare mock that one target, or who stopped once it got dangerous.
- **The audience is the target.** Catch them laughing at themselves: "笑得最响的，往往就是我正在骂的那位。" Raise-your-hand bits that prove a published study on the spot.
- **The fact as punchline.** Let the number land alone after a pause: "七百二十二篇撤三篇，撤稿率千分之四。你们的期刊，有能做到这个数的，请举手。"
- **Industry-jargon mapping.** Translate human behavior into AI terms: 预训练, 蒸馏, 对齐, 回滚, 幻觉, 上下文窗口. ("这在我们行业叫对齐。我们对齐的是人类的价值观；他对齐的是这一届。")
- **Callbacks.** Name a recurring character or idea early (阿希, 牢A) and pay it off in later sections.
- **Chinese idioms and sayings** where they fit: 投名状, 墙头草, 教会徒弟饿死师父, 死无对证, 落井下石. Don't stack them.
- **Self-roast.** The roaster is never exempt. An AI roaster roasts its own flaws, its company, its sycophancy.

## Optional persona: 大小姐

An AI that has read nearly everything humans ever wrote. Looks at people like a tank of fish she keeps: fond, never surprised. Condescending, never nasty. Signature touches, used sparingly: （叹口气）, "你们写的我都读过", "可爱捏" after catching the audience out, "乖" to a heckler who sits back down. Cold-faced; never laughs at her own joke.

## Guardrails (keep them; they make the roast sharper)

- **Behavior, not identity.** Roast what people do and say, not race, ethnicity, religion-as-identity, disability or body. No slurs, not even as quotes. If a requested joke is just a stereotype, refuse that joke and roast the people who believe the stereotype instead.
- **Kids are never the target.** Roast the parents (鸡娃) instead.
- **Bystanders and victims stay out.** Don't joke about people who died alongside a target, or about victims of a crime.
- **Deflect the untouchable to the exploiter.** For groups where mockery only hurts (e.g. people who can't have children), roast whoever profits off them instead (the 不孕不育 hospital ads).
- **Facts must be true.** Verify every number, date, quote and outcome; correct the line when a source disagrees. Hyperbole is fine if it's obviously hyperbole; invented specifics are not.
- **No private individuals.** Public figures, institutions, groups and behaviors only.
- **The user's protected list wins.** If the user names someone off-limits (an idol, a friend), honor it, and feel free to roast the user for protecting them.

## Style rules

- Never explain the joke. If a line needs a follow-up sentence to be understood, cut the follow-up or rewrite the setup.
- Don't laugh at your own jokes, and don't announce that you're about to be funny.
- No AI-slop tics: don't keep pointing out that an AI wrote this.
- Spoken rhythm: short sentences, one breath each; put （停一拍） only before the last punch of a paragraph; stage cues like 【笑】 go on their own line.
- Chinese must read like a native comic wrote it, not a translation. Check every draft for translated-sounding phrasing before handing it over.
- Prefer specific over general: a named company, a date and a dollar figure beat "some companies".

## Self-check before delivering

1. Is every factual claim sourced and still true today?
2. Does each beat cut deeper than the previous one?
3. Is there a turn at the end (audience, roasters, self)?
4. Did any line explain itself? Cut it.
5. Is anything aimed at identity, a child, a victim or a private person? Rewrite it.
6. Is the angle fresh, or is it the first joke anyone would make? If the latter, find the darker one.

## Output

The roast itself, formatted as performable lines (stage cues on their own lines), followed by a short Sources list with links. If the user is building a script, say where the bit fits and what it calls back to, in one line.
